# LumaPair Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

LumaPair is developed by Dongsheng Hu. This policy describes the LumaPair macOS app and support communications.

## Local image processing

LumaPair reads FITS files you choose and processes image previews on your Mac. It does not upload your images, FITS headers, filenames, file paths, or image data to the developer or an external service. Original files are not modified by the app's viewing tools.

## Information collected by the app

LumaPair does not include advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, account registration, or cloud synchronization. The app does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. Its current sandbox configuration does not enable network access.

The app does not provide saved comparison sessions. Selected images and display state are used for the current session. macOS may separately manage application state and diagnostic information under your system settings.

## File access

The app accesses files you select through macOS file selection or file-opening actions. It reads file information needed to validate supported images and display previews. It does not scan your photo library or upload your documents.

Apple may process purchase or diagnostic information under its own privacy policies and your device settings. Pages opened outside LumaPair, including GitHub support pages and the App Store, are governed by their respective providers' policies. This policy does not describe data processing by separate companion apps.

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
