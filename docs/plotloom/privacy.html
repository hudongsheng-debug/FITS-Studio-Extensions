PlotLoom Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

PlotLoom is developed by Dongsheng Hu. This policy describes the PlotLoom macOS app and support correspondence.

Data processed on your Mac

PlotLoom reads files you choose and processes their numerical contents locally to display scientific charts. It does not upload your data files, variable names, table contents, charts, or exported figures to the developer or to an app-operated server.

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs. The developer does not collect personal data through the app.

Local files and temporary data

The app uses macOS file dialogs to open selected files and save exports. Exported files remain at the location you choose. Reading a source file does not modify it; choose a separate export filename if you wish to preserve an existing output file.

Selected data may be cached in memory during use. Gzip-compressed FITS files may be decompressed into temporary local files. The app attempts to remove these temporary files when their associated file session is released; an interruption or crash may leave temporary data for later system cleanup. These files are not sent to the developer.

If you choose a location managed by iCloud Drive or another file-sync provider, that provider may sync files according to your settings and its own privacy policy. PlotLoom does not operate that sync service.

Support correspondence

If you email hudongsheng356@gmail.com, the developer receives the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots, and attachments. It is used to respond to your request and investigate reported problems. Email providers process messages for delivery and storage. Please share only files and information you intend to provide; a small synthetic example is usually sufficient for troubleshooting.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and related follow-up. You may request deletion by contacting the same address, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Apple services

App Store distribution, purchases, and any operating-system diagnostic sharing are handled under Apple's policies and your settings. This policy does not replace Apple's privacy policy. PlotLoom does not add its own in-app telemetry service.

Updates and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

For privacy questions or requests, contact hudongsheng356@gmail.com.
